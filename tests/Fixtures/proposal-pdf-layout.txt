                             DETAILED ACTIVITY PROPOSAL
Title:              FRESHIVAL 2026
Theme:              Where New Beginnings Meet the Beat
Date:               October 22, 2026
Proponent:          Student Government
Budget:             P 52,050.00

I.      Rationale
Every school year, the Student Government welcomes the new students with a festival of music, talent
and friendship.
This year the festival also recognizes first-year students who show leadership.

II.     Objectives
The celebration specifically aims to:
1.      Welcome the first-year students to the University;
2.      Showcase the talents of the new students; and
3.      Recognize emerging student leaders.

III.   Methods & Approaches
The program will be held at the University Gymnasium with a pageant, performances and an
awarding.
